Who Would Benefit From a Safe Money & Retirement Consultation?

  • People approaching retirement, or already retired, who are seriously considering their options
  • Individuals with money in a 401(k), 403(b), TSP, IRA, CD, savings, annuity, or other retirement account
  • People considering a rollover after retiring, changing jobs, or leaving an employer
  • Individuals concerned about protecting their savings from market losses
  • People interested in reliable retirement income
  • Individuals exploring annuities or other safe-money strategies
  • People ready to evaluate whether one of these strategies may be right for them

How to Prepare for Your Consultation

  • Have your most recent retirement or account statement available so we can work from your actual current balance rather than an estimate.
  • Know when you plan to retire, or when you retired if you are already retired.
  • Be prepared to discuss what you want this money to accomplish — such as protecting principal, creating retirement income, reducing market exposure, or pursuing long-term growth.
  • Have information available about other retirement accounts or assets that may be relevant to the discussion.
  • Think about whether you expect to need income from this money and approximately when you would need it.
  • Have any specific questions or concerns you would like addressed.
